## {% href_link 🪟 | Fragile Blocks %}

{% symlink fragile_blocks, Fragile blocks %} are blocks that can't withstand a strong physical impact and break from it.

`Fragile blocks`{: .mc-gold} break when:
- Something lands on them after falling more than 12 blocks.
    - A shorter fall that deals at least one heart of damage also has a 60% chance to break the block.
    - Landing on a `fragile block`{: .mc-gold} reduces the fall damage by 20%.
- A fast-flying {% symlink arrows, Arrow %} or {% symlink trident, Trident %} hits them. The projectile flies on through the broken block, slightly slowed down.
- A thrown {% symlink rock, Rock %} (of any kind) hits them.
