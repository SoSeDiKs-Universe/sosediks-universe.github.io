## {% href_link 🔥 | Hot Items %}

{% symlink hot_items, Hot items %} are items too hot to be touched with bare hands.

Without {% symlink gloves, Gloves %}, grabbing a `hot item`{: .mc-gold} burns: the player catches fire for 3 seconds and drops the item. This happens when:
- Picking it up from the ground.
- Taking it in the inventory.
- Switching to it on the hotbar.

Filling a {% symlink glass_bottle, Glass Bottle %} underwater while holding a `hot item`{: .mc-gold} in the other hand still replenishes air, but the water boils away: the bottle stays empty, and may burst (50% chance).
